## Confirmed from pinned source and component checks

- Argo 3.3.7 uses `view=tree|network|list|pods`, `node=group/kind/namespace/name/0`, and `tab=summary|events|logs|manifest|diff` in the Application URL. Conditions, operation, and History use `conditions=true`, `operation=true`, and `rollback=0`. The observer uses these signals. Source: `ui/src/app/applications/components/application-details/application-details.tsx` and `resource-details/resource-details.tsx` at tag `v3.3.7`.
- Argo uses `GET /applications/{name}/resource-tree`, `/managed-resources`, `/resource`, and `/events`; `POST /sync`, `/rollback`, and `/resource`; and `DELETE /operation` and `/resource` under `/api/v1`. Resource details prefetch data even while Summary is open, so a tab visit is credited from the URL. Source: `ui/src/app/shared/services/applications-service.ts` at tag `v3.3.7`.
- Gitea's internal routes have no `/gitea` prefix, while its public links do. The gateway strips the prefix; the login handoff handles Gitea's CSRF cookie path. Packaged ttyd 1.7.7 supports `-b /terminal` and exposes `/terminal/ws`.
- Local image builds, nginx syntax, Go, Python, Node, shell, YAML, Helm rendering, and deployment contract checks pass.
